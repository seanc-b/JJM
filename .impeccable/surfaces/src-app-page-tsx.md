# Home page surface brief

Scope: `/` (src/app/page.tsx). Mode: Persuade. The desktop mockup from the client's designer is pinned and binding (`../Assets/Design from Anne/JJM Website LANDING vf.pdf`); no direction roll was run because the brief pins the visual world. Build path: code-led.

Audience and job: B2B evaluators (property managers, commercial owners) and existing clients checking services, credentials and contact details. Proof: real photography of real installations, credentials (BCWWA since 2007, Navien, licensed gas contractor), same-day service.

Constraints: Work Sans only; navy #17355d, blue #3f74b7, text #3e4d5d, white; copy as set in the mockup with the confirmed fixes (Gas fitting, "Plumbing" nav label, 20 years, Address). Placeholders, clearly marked, for partner logos, news thumbnails/videos and menu icons. WCAG 2.2 AA; every motion has a reduced-motion alternative. The alternating-image scroll effect in the service sections is deferred; sections are structured to take it later.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A mechanical contractor's portfolio that earns trust by showing the work at full bleed, in the designer's exact composition: photo-led banners in navy, white editorial sections between them. It refuses the lead-gen landing page of stat tiles, testimonial cards and sticky CTAs.

OWN-WORLD: Work Sans caps for headings, navy ground on photos (multiply-tinted), white sections with brand-blue section icons and 12%-opacity oversized background icons, hairline rules in blue, outlined pill buttons, dotted dividers. Motion is the brand's second material: Lenis smooth scroll, a scroll-velocity marquee, clip-path and parallax reveals, hover that lifts photos and slides button labels.

STORY: The visitor sees a real plant room on arrival, reads what JJM does in one scroll, sees that the photos are JJM's own jobs, and reaches a phone number or email without looking for it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Navien plant-room photo tinted navy, white logo top-left, five nav links and mail/phone icons top-right under a hairline. Headline "BUILT ON TRUST, DRIVEN BY CUSTOMER SATISFACTION" right-of-centre at 46px, outlined "CONTACT US" pill beneath it. Bottom-left "EMERGENCY 604.422.0204", bottom-right "STAY CONNECTED jjmmech". Primary action: Contact Us.

FORM: The designer's mockup, reproduced. The roll was run for the record (assigned index 6 of a grounded list) and its assignment is overridden by the brief-pinned mockup, which beats the roll. seed:cb00b31872f56c90

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the services marquee reads scroll velocity and direction, drifting faster and reversing with the visitor's scroll. Section photos reveal with a clip-path wipe and slow parallax; headline lines rise in sequence once.

Unresolved: partner logo files, news content and videos, menu icons, hosting.
